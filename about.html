Our Story

Custom furniture and interiors, built from first visit to final installation

R&G Homes GH is an Accra-based custom furniture and interior design company. We design, produce and install bespoke interiors for homes and businesses across Greater Accra and beyond, using skilled craftsmanship together with 3D design and rendering so every space is built to the client's vision, taste and budget.

How We Work

From first call to final walkthrough

We manage every stage of your project ourselves, so you deal with one team from start to finish.

Enquiry and consultation

You get in touch and we talk through what you have in mind.

Site visit

We inspect the space, take measurements, and gather a detailed brief covering your requirements, style and budget.

3D design and rendering

We produce a full visual of the proposed design for you to review, before anything is built.

Design sign-off

You approve every detail before production begins.

Production

Fabrication and finishing at our Kwabenya workshop.

Delivery and installation

Our team installs and completes the project on site.

Final walkthrough

We go through the finished work with you and hand over.

Craft

Materials and finishes

Material choice is made project by project, based on the structure, the finish and your budget.

Materials

MDF, HDF, plywood and solid wood, chosen to give each space the best balance of durability and appearance.

Finishes

Spray paint, high-gloss lacquer and matte finishes, applied by our own team.

Upholstery

Upholstery and fabric work is done in our workshop, alongside the joinery.

What We Do

Full interiors or individual pieces

Turnkey interior projects

We manage the whole interior, from design concept through to final furnishing.

Furniture and fittings

Custom pieces and installations delivered within an existing space, such as kitchens, wardrobes, beds and dining sets.

Timelines

What to expect

Kitchen installationsabout 3 to 4 weeks
Wardrobe installationsabout 2 to 3 weeks
